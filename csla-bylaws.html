Bylaws of the Coronado Surf Lifesaving Association

Article I: Name and Purpose

Section 1. Name

The name of this organization shall be the Coronado Surf Lifesaving Association (hereinafter referred to as the "Association").

Section 2. Purpose

The Coronado Surf Lifesaving Association is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or corresponding section of any future federal tax code. The specific purposes of this Association are:

  1. To promote water safety and prevent drowning through education and training, with a focus on ocean environments.
  2. To support and enhance the Coronado Junior Lifeguards program, fostering the development of future lifeguards and promoting water safety awareness among youth.
  3. To advance the professional standards and capabilities of lifeguards in Coronado and surrounding areas.
  4. To encourage and support participation in lifeguard competitions for current professional lifeguards, promoting skill enhancement, physical fitness, and camaraderie within the lifeguarding community.
  5. To foster cooperation among lifeguard agencies and professionals.
  6. To advocate for policies and practices that enhance public safety in beach and aquatic environments.

Section 3. Support for Junior Lifeguards Program

The Association shall support the Coronado Junior Lifeguards program through the following initiatives:

  1. Mentorship Program: Establish a mentorship program pairing experienced Association members with Junior Lifeguards to provide guidance, support, and career advice.
  2. Equipment Support: Allocate a portion of the Association's budget to provide necessary equipment and resources for the Junior Lifeguards program.
  3. Training Assistance: Offer additional training sessions and workshops to Junior Lifeguards, focusing on advanced lifesaving techniques, first aid, and ocean safety.
  4. Scholarship Fund: Create and maintain a scholarship fund to support Junior Lifeguards who wish to pursue further education or certification in lifesaving and related fields.
  5. Community Outreach: Collaborate with local schools and youth organizations to promote the Junior Lifeguards program and water safety awareness.
  6. Competition Support: Assist in organizing and sponsoring Junior Lifeguard competitions to promote skill development and camaraderie.
  7. Leadership Development: Provide opportunities for senior Junior Lifeguards to develop leadership skills through assisting with program administration and peer training.
  8. Parent Education: Offer educational sessions for parents of Junior Lifeguards to enhance their understanding of water safety and support their children's involvement in the program.
  9. Transition Program: Develop a structured program to support the transition of Junior Lifeguards to full-fledged lifeguards, including guidance on certification processes and job opportunities.
  10. Annual Review: Conduct an annual review of the Association's support for the Junior Lifeguards program, seeking feedback from participants, parents, and program administrators to continually improve and expand support initiatives.

Article II: Offices

Section 1. Principal Office

The principal office of the Association shall be located in Coronado, California. The Association may have such other offices as the Board of Directors may determine or as the affairs of the Association may require from time to time.

Article III: Membership

Section 1. Eligibility

Membership shall be open to all individuals who support the purpose of the Association and meet the criteria established for membership categories.

Section 2. Membership Categories

The Association shall have the following membership categories:

  1. Active Members: Currently employed ocean lifeguards in Coronado and surrounding coastal areas.
  2. Associate Members: Former ocean lifeguards, lifeguards from other regions, and individuals involved in beach and aquatic safety.
  3. Student Members: Students enrolled in lifeguard training programs.
  4. Honorary Members: Individuals who have made significant contributions to lifeguarding or water safety.

Section 3. Dues

  1. Determination of Dues: The Board of Directors shall determine the annual dues for each membership category. The Board may adjust dues amounts annually to reflect the financial needs of the Association.
  2. Dues Structure: Dues may vary among membership categories to reflect the different levels of engagement and benefits associated with each category. The Board shall strive to keep dues affordable while ensuring the financial stability of the Association.
  3. Payment Schedule: Annual dues shall be payable on January 1st of each year. New members joining after July 1st may be eligible for prorated dues for their first year of membership, as determined by the Board.
  4. Multi-Year Options: The Board may offer multi-year membership options at a discounted rate to encourage long-term commitment to the Association.
  5. Late Fees and Grace Period: Members who fail to pay dues by March 1st may be subject to a late fee. A grace period until April 1st may be granted, after which unpaid memberships may be suspended.
  6. Hardship Provisions: The Board may establish a process for members experiencing financial hardship to request a temporary reduction or waiver of dues.
  7. Refunds: Dues are generally non-refundable. However, the Board may consider prorated refunds in exceptional circumstances.
  8. Use of Dues: Membership dues shall be used to further the purposes of the Association as outlined in Article I, Section 2 of these Bylaws.
  9. Transparency: The Treasurer shall provide an annual report to the membership detailing how dues are allocated to support the Association's activities.

Section 4. Voting Rights

Active Members in good standing shall have the right to vote in Association elections and on matters submitted to the membership for approval.

Article IV: Board of Directors

Section 1. General Powers

The affairs of the Association shall be managed by its Board of Directors.

Section 2. Number and Qualifications

The Board shall consist of no fewer than five (5) and no more than fifteen (15) directors.

Section 3. Election and Term of Office

Directors shall be elected by the Board at the annual meeting. Each director shall serve a two-year term, with terms staggered so that approximately half of the directors are elected each year.

Section 4. Meetings

The Board of Directors shall meet at least quarterly. Special meetings may be called by the President or by any three directors.

Section 5. Quorum

A majority of the Board of Directors shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

Article V: Officers

Section 1. Officers

The officers of the Association shall be a President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer.

Section 2. Election and Term of Office

Officers shall be elected by the Active Members annually from among the Board and Active Members. Each officer shall serve a one-year term.

Section 3. Duties

  1. President: The President shall be the principal executive officer of the Association and shall supervise all of the business and affairs of the Association.
  2. Vice President: The Vice President shall perform the duties of the President in the absence or disability of the President.
  3. Secretary: The Secretary shall keep the minutes of meetings and maintain the records of the Association, including actions of directors and committees of directors. The secretary shall also give notices for all meetings of directors and committees, and certify any board resolution.
  4. Treasurer: The Treasurer shall have charge and custody of all funds of the Association. The Treasurer shall oversee budgeting, financial reports, and tax and information returns.

Article VI: Committees

Section 1. Standing Committees

The Association shall have the following standing committees:

  1. Education and Training Committee
  2. Public Relations Committee
  3. Membership Committee
  4. Junior Lifeguards Committee

Section 2. Ad Hoc Committees

The Board of Directors may create ad hoc committees as needed.

Article VII: Finances

Section 1. Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of the Association shall be the calendar year.

Section 2. Books and Records

The Association shall keep correct and complete books and records of account.

Section 3. Audits

The financial records of the Association shall be audited annually by an independent auditor.

Article VIII: Indemnification

The Association shall indemnify its directors, officers, and employees to the fullest extent permitted by California and federal law.

Article IX: Amendments

These Bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the Active Members present at any regular meeting or special meeting called for that purpose, provided that written notice of the proposed amendment has been sent to all Active Members at least 30 days prior to the meeting.

Article X: Dissolution

Upon the dissolution of the Association, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or corresponding section of any future federal tax code, or shall be distributed to the federal government, or to a state or local government, for a public purpose.
