# Bylaws of the Coronado Surf Lifesaving Association

## Article I: Name and Purpose

### Section 1. Name
The name of this organization shall be the Coronado Surf Lifesaving Association (hereinafter referred to as the "Association").

### Section 2. Purpose
The Coronado Surf Lifesaving Association is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or corresponding section of any future federal tax code. The specific purposes of this Association are:

a) To promote water safety and prevent drowning through education and training, with a focus on surf environments.
b) To advance the professional standards and capabilities of surf lifeguards in Coronado and surrounding areas.
c) To foster cooperation among lifeguard agencies and professionals in coastal communities.
d) To advocate for policies and practices that enhance public safety in beach and surf environments.

## Article II: Offices

### Section 1. Principal Office
The principal office of the Association shall be located in Coronado, California. The Association may have such other offices as the Board of Directors may determine or as the affairs of the Association may require from time to time.

## Article III: Membership

### Section 1. Eligibility
Membership shall be open to all individuals who support the purpose of the Association and meet the criteria established for membership categories.

### Section 2. Membership Categories
The Association shall have the following membership categories:
a) Active Members: Currently employed surf lifeguards in Coronado and surrounding coastal areas.
b) Associate Members: Former surf lifeguards, lifeguards from other regions, and individuals involved in beach and aquatic safety.
c) Student Members: Students enrolled in surf lifeguard training programs.
d) Honorary Members: Individuals who have made significant contributions to surf lifesaving or water safety.

### Section 3. Dues
The Board of Directors shall determine the annual dues for each membership category.

### Section 4. Voting Rights
Active Members in good standing shall have the right to vote in Association elections and on matters submitted to the membership for approval.

## Article IV: Board of Directors

### Section 1. General Powers
The affairs of the Association shall be managed by its Board of Directors.

### Section 2. Number and Qualifications
The Board shall consist of no fewer than five (5) and no more than fifteen (15) directors. Directors must be Active Members of the Association.

### Section 3. Election and Term of Office
Directors shall be elected by the Active Members at the annual meeting. Each director shall serve a two-year term, with terms staggered so that approximately half of the directors are elected each year.

### Section 4. Meetings
The Board of Directors shall meet at least quarterly. Special meetings may be called by the President or by any three directors.

### Section 5. Quorum
A majority of the Board of Directors shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

## Article V: Officers

### Section 1. Officers
The officers of the Association shall be a President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer.

### Section 2. Election and Term of Office
Officers shall be elected by the Board of Directors from among its members. Each officer shall serve a one-year term.

### Section 3. Duties
a) President: The President shall be the principal executive officer of the Association and shall supervise all of the business and affairs of the Association.
b) Vice President: The Vice President shall perform the duties of the President in the absence of the President.
c) Secretary: The Secretary shall keep the minutes of meetings and maintain the records of the Association.
d) Treasurer: The Treasurer shall have charge and custody of all funds of the Association.

## Article VI: Committees

### Section 1. Standing Committees
The Association shall have the following standing committees:
a) Education and Training Committee
b) Public Relations Committee
c) Membership Committee
d) Surf Safety Committee

### Section 2. Ad Hoc Committees
The Board of Directors may create ad hoc committees as needed.

## Article VII: Finances

### Section 1. Fiscal Year
The fiscal year of the Association shall be the calendar year.

### Section 2. Books and Records
The Association shall keep correct and complete books and records of account.

### Section 3. Audits
The financial records of the Association shall be audited annually by an independent auditor.

## Article VIII: Indemnification

The Association shall indemnify its directors, officers, and employees to the fullest extent permitted by California and federal law.

## Article IX: Amendments

These Bylaws may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the Active Members present at any regular meeting or special meeting called for that purpose, provided that written notice of the proposed amendment has been sent to all Active Members at least 30 days prior to the meeting.

## Article X: Dissolution

Upon the dissolution of the Association, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or corresponding section of any future federal tax code, or shall be distributed to the federal government, or to a state or local government, for a public purpose.
